# RMFG — LTL Freight Shipping

Orders that exceed standard parcel limits require LTL (Less Than Truckload)
freight shipping. If any of the following thresholds are met, the order will
ship via freight and a shipping quote will be provided separately.

## LTL Freight Thresholds

An order requires freight shipping if any of these conditions are true:

| Condition | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Total order weight | Over 200 lbs |
| Any single part weight | Over 100 lbs |
| Longest dimension of any part | 60" or more |
| Two largest dimensions of any part | 48" or more AND 30" or more |

## LTL Freight Details

- Flat rate of $200 per order
- Estimated transit time of 3 business days after production is complete
- Freight shipments only ship out Monday through Friday
- Deliveries are made Monday through Friday only — no weekend deliveries
- Expedited production can be combined with LTL freight to reduce overall
  lead time

When freight shipping is required, you will see a notice during checkout and
the $200 freight surcharge will be added automatically.
